A note on this demo. The panel above is a placeholder, not a real interpreter. Producing genuine signed video requires a qualified Deaf or hearing interpreter and cannot be synthesised, so this suite marks the position and size an interpreter must occupy rather than faking the content.

For real projects: commission a qualified interpreter, film them against a plain contrasting background, and give the viewer a control to show, hide and enlarge the panel.
