Book review: Gitanjali

Rabindranath Tagore's collection was first published in Bangla in 1910. The opening line is one of the most quoted in the language:

আমার সোনার বাংলা, আমি তোমায় ভালোবাসি।

— Rabindranath Tagore, Gitanjali

The word গীতাঞ্জলি translates roughly as "song offering".
